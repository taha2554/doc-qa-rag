# Northwind Studio – Employee Handbook

This handbook is a made-up example document used to demo the question answering system.

## Working hours

Our core hours are 10:00–16:00 (Istanbul time), Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can organise your day freely, as long as you work 40 hours per week on average. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without asking first.

## Remote work

Everyone can work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need approval from your team lead and are reviewed every six months. If you work from abroad for more than 30 days in a calendar year, talk to HR first because of tax rules.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 20 working days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. After five years at the company this increases to 25 days. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; they must be used before 31 March.

Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than three days.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, message your team lead before 10:00. For absences longer than two consecutive days a doctor's note is required. Sick days do not reduce your annual leave.

## Equipment

New employees receive a laptop and a 27" monitor. You can expense up to 3,000 TL per year for home office equipment such as a chair or headphones. Keep the receipt and submit it within 30 days.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 6,000 TL for courses, books and conferences. Conference travel is covered separately if your talk is accepted.

## Security

Use the company password manager for all work accounts and enable two-factor authentication everywhere it is available. Report a lost or stolen laptop to it-help@northwind.example within 24 hours.
