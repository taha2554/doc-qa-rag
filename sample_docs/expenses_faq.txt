Expenses FAQ (example document)

Q: How do I submit an expense?
A: Upload a photo of the receipt in the finance app, choose a category and add a one-line description. Expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.

Q: What is the limit for client dinners?
A: Up to 750 TL per person including drinks. Anything above that needs approval from your manager before the dinner.

Q: Are taxis covered?
A: Taxis are covered after 22:00 or when carrying equipment. Otherwise please use public transport.

Q: What happens if I lose a receipt?
A: Fill in the missing receipt form. This can be used at most twice per year.
